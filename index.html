Recognition

Invited as Chief Guest at YEHU Members Welfare Association AGM 2025 — April 24th 2026, Muslim Luhar Wadha Jamat Hall, Mombasa.

What Happened

A landmark integrated empowerment event on International Women's Day — bringing together Equity Group Holdings, Equity Afya, Jomvu Women in Fisheries, and Coastal Women in Fisheries CBO to deliver financial literacy, health awareness, and marine enterprise planning in a single community engagement.

Outcomes
  • Delivered an integrated empowerment engagement combining financial literacy (Equity Group Holdings) and health awareness & access (Equity Afya)
  • Strengthened trust and engagement with coastal women groups
  • Demonstrated a replicable multi-sector partnership model for women empowerment
  • Opened pathways for structured financial literacy programs, health outreach initiatives, and future asset-financing discussions including marine equipment and boats
  • Elevated visibility of Making Waves among institutional partners as a credible platform for community-based interventions

This collaboration proved that financial institutions and health bodies are willing to engage coastal women — when the right intermediary creates the space, trust, and structure to do so.

Next Steps
  • Roll out targeted financial literacy training across 10 Beach Management Units
  • Explore financing models for boat acquisition and marine business development
  • Expand health outreach initiatives within women fisheries groups via Equity Afya
  • Scale the model regionally across East and Central Africa
